Privacy

Overlap · last updated 22 November 2026

In short: Overlap keeps only what it needs to show you and your friends when you are in the same place. Only friends you accept see your plans. We never sell your data and there are no ads.

What we keep

  • Your email, to sign you in with a code.
  • Your name, username, WhatsApp number and photo, if you add them, so friends know who you are and can message you.
  • Your home city and your trips (city, dates, emoji, note). Trips marked Private are visible only to you.
  • Your friends and requests, so we know who can see your plans.
  • A notification token, if you turn on alerts, so we can tell you when you overlap.

What we do not keep

  • Your location: "Use my location" is used once on your phone to fill in a city, and is not stored.
  • Your phone calendar: "From my calendar" reads it on your phone only when you tap it, to suggest trips. Nothing is sent unless you add a trip.
  • Your contacts.

Who sees what

Friends you accept see your name, photo, home city and trips that are not private. People who are not your friends can only find you by name or username to send a request.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and photos) and alerts are sent through Expo and Apple. They process data only to run Overlap.

Blocking and reports

You can block or report anyone from their profile. Reports are read by the person who runs Overlap.

Delete your data

In Overlap, open Settings and tap Delete my account. Your account, trips, friends and photo are deleted at once.
