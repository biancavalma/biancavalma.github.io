How do I add a friend?

Tap the smiley with the magnifying glass at the top, search their name or @username, or send them your invite link.

How do I add a trip?

Tap the first and last day in the calendar, or the + button, then type the city.

Can I hide a trip?

Yes: switch on Private when you add or edit it. Only you will see it.

How do I stop sharing with someone?

Open their profile in Friends and tap Stop sharing, Block or Report.

How do I delete my account?

Settings, then Delete my account.
